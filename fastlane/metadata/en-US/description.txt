Stromkreis is the open platform by energy communities for energy communities. This app connects members of an Austrian energy community (EEG) to their Stromkreis gateway at home through the Stromkreis Cloud.

Features:

* Live view of your PV system, home battery and grid feed-in
* Forecast-driven battery management: charge your storage when it benefits the community
* Remote access to your gateway from anywhere via the Stromkreis Cloud (https://hac.stromkreis.net)
* Simple setup by QR code or setup link
* Natively supports iPhone and iPad

Stromkreis is non-profit and open source. Learn more at https://stromkreis.net

The Stromkreis gateway is built on openHAB; this app is based on the openHAB iOS client (Eclipse Public License 2.0).
